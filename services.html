How it works

Three steps to your report

Call and book

Ring +91-96614 27034 or +91-70041 30399 and tell us which test you need. We'll confirm preparation instructions and pricing.

Give your sample

Walk in to either centre, or let a trained phlebotomist collect the sample at your home — home collection is available on every package.
